How multiplayer works (v0.9)
Client sends state at 20Hz with performance.now() timestamps; the server keeps a per-connection clock-offset EMA and a ~1s position ring buffer, so it can rewind any target to the shooter's fire moment and reject hits that miss the ray (radius 1.0 at eye height). Shots and hits bypass the 30ms throttle; damage is clamped to 100/hit, 12 hits/sec. Remote players render as cylinder+sphere markers named "P"+id. The design decisions I'd call out as good: validating movement with the server's arrival clock instead of client vt (netcode.js:319 explains why), reservation expiry for players who matchmake but never connect, and hibernation-safe state restore.
