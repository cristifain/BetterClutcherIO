About Clutcher.io

Clutcher.io is a free tactical first-person shooter that runs right in your web browser. There is nothing to download and no account to make: open the page and play. Your settings, progress and items are saved in your browser.

Play Clutcher.io now

Game modes

  • Bomb Defuse — two teams, round by round. One side plants the bomb at a bomb site and the other side has to stop them or defuse it. Every round opens with a short buy phase, paid for with the money you earned in the rounds before.
  • Team Deathmatch — nonstop action: you respawn after every death, and the team with the most kills wins.

Matches are played against computer-controlled bots.

Maps

  • Oasis — a sunlit desert town of markets, courtyards and narrow lanes.
  • Dune — a sand-swept town of long corridors, tunnels and open sites.

How to play

Pick a mode, a map and a side, then press PLAY. Aim with the mouse and work with your team to win the round.

  • W A S D move, Space jump, Ctrl or C crouch, Shift walk quietly
  • Left click fire, right click scope or alternate fire, R reload
  • B buy menu, E plant or defuse the bomb, Q last weapon, G drop weapon, F inspect weapon
  • Tab scoreboard, N cases, Esc pause

Every key can be changed in Settings. On phones and tablets the game shows touch controls instead.

Features

  • Loadouts — choose which weapons appear in your buy menu.
  • Buy menu — spend your round money on weapons, armor and grenades.
  • Daily and weekly challenges — complete them to earn coins.
  • Cases and inventory — open cases with coins to collect weapon skins, then equip them from your inventory.
  • Automatic graphics — the game adjusts its quality to run smoothly on slower devices, and you can fine-tune it in Settings.

Nothing in Clutcher.io costs real money. Coins are only earned by playing.
